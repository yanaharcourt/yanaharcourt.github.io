The practice

A small practice with its own lab

Patrick St Dental is on Patrick Street in Dublin 8. We take digital images on site and make crowns and other restorations in our own CAD/CAM lab, so more of your treatment fits into fewer visits.

Cold plasma therapy

Bleeding gums are often the first sign of periodontitis. Cold plasma is a non-invasive option, including for patients who can't take antibiotics.

Auto-Steril disinfection

Our dental chairs disinfect themselves automatically, including protection against Legionella.

Digital imaging and CAD/CAM

Scans and in-house lab work cut the waiting time between your first visit and the finished crown or veneer.

Implants and prosthetics

For partly or fully missing teeth. Osseointegration succeeds in more than 95% of cases, depending on hygiene, bone quality and general health.
