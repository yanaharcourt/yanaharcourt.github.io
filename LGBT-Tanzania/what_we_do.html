Our Programs

What We Do

LGBT Voice Tanzania provides a wide range of services to support the LGBTQ+ community in Tanzania — reducing isolation, helping people feel safe, and enabling people to flourish.

A wide range of services

We believe in a fair and equal society where all LGBTQ+ people can achieve their full potential. Our work is as vital and urgent as ever — the LGBTQ+ community still faces health inequalities, persecution and discrimination.

Every year we give a voice, provide help and offer hope to 200 people. Our services and activities include:

  • Support groups
  • Legal aid
  • Face-to-face counselling
  • Sexual health program
  • Substance-misuse project
  • Security training
  • Trauma counselling
  • Temporary shelter
  • Emergency support
  • Policy advocacy
  • Guides and resources
  • National website

Our Work

Five interconnected programs that together address the full range of challenges facing LGBT+ people in Tanzania.

01
Political Advocacy

Political Advocacy

Through political work for LGBT human rights in Tanzania, we seek to improve the understanding and recognition of an inclusive and diverse perception of gender identity and sexual orientation.

  • Protecting and promoting LGBT human rights — we advocate for recognition, acceptance, respect and protection of LGBTQ+ people as guaranteed in Tanzania's constitution.
  • Networking and empowerment of community based organisations (CBOs) at grassroots level to mobilise support for LGBT rights.
  • Monitoring policies and legislation that infringe upon LGBTQ+ human rights and sharing that information with the global community.
  • Establishing community networks to promote and advocate for LGBT human rights.
  • Training in leadership and advocacy for public advocates on LGBTQ+ human rights — educating local leaders on how to combat anti-LGBT ignorance and prejudice.
  • Legal and policy reform — including the repeal of penal code section 154 that criminalises homosexuality.

02
Healthcare Access

Health Care Access

Millions of LGBTQIA+ people in Tanzania do not have access to quality health services, including HIV prevention, treatment, care, and support, due to prejudice based on gender identity and sexual orientation.

Free LGBT Clinic Project

LGBT Voice works in partnership with health providers who run LGBT+ friendly facilities in Dar es Salaam that provide the following free quality health services:

HIV testing
TB screening
Treatment care
Free condoms
HIV-prevention education
Physical & emotional support

Mental Health & Wellbeing

LGBT Voice Tanzania provides free trauma-informed care to individuals impacted by unjust arrests, detention and torture — helping them cope with the emotional response caused by traumatic incidents.

03
Crisis Support

Crisis Aid & Emergency Support

  • Eviction support — when a person is evicted by a landlord because of anti-LGBTQ+ prejudice, we negotiate for additional time and help find new accommodations or provide temporary shelter.
  • Family rejection — when parents or relatives throw a person out of their home, we help them find a new place to live and avoid homelessness.
  • Education protection — when a LGBT student is expelled from school for discriminatory reasons, we help them obtain private classes and sit for exams as private candidates.
  • Asylum assistance — if the situation becomes intolerable, we help LGBT people obtain asylum in other countries.
  • Employment claims — we file claims and attempt to gain compensation for employees fired solely because of anti-LGBT discrimination.
  • Police intervention — we have forced the police to drop false charges filed against several gay men simply because they were gay.

04
Legal Aid

Free Legal Aid

LGBTQ+ persons in Tanzania constantly face stigma and discrimination, harassment and arbitrary arrests, alienation from family and faith, and lack of access to social services including health, justice, housing, education, and dignified livelihoods.

LGBT Voice Tanzania hires lawyers who provide free legal aid to LGBTQ+ people who are victims of unjust arrest, torture, detention and violence.

Our lawyers help file claims and attempt to gain compensation for employees who were fired from their jobs solely because of anti-LGBTQ+ discrimination.

05
Education & Media

Education Through Media

LGBT Voice Tanzania acts as both watchdog and advocate to the media — empowering real people to share their stories, while holding the media accountable for the words and images they present.

As more Tanzanians see our stories in newspapers, on our website and social media, or in entertainment media, they will grow to respect and understand our community, paving the way to full equality.

Educating Community Leadership

In Tanzania, widespread misinformation about homosexuality encourages oppression and violence. We must educate and equip community leaders to combat this cycle of blame and shame. In 2010, we organised training for more than 30 local leaders.
