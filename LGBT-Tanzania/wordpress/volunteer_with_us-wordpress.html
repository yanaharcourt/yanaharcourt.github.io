Our Volunteers

The Backbone of Our Work

People Who Make Change Possible

LGBT Voice Tanzania's all-volunteer network is made up of people from a wide array of backgrounds, experiences, identities, and skill sets. Our volunteers work on the front lines in the fight for LGBTQ+ equality — in healthcare advocacy, community outreach, legal support, and beyond.

We invest in our volunteers through leadership development and recognition programs, offering workshops, conferences, and direct staff support to ensure our leaders become the strongest and most effective advocates they can be.

No matter how much time you're able to give, LGBT Voice Tanzania has something meaningful for you. We match you with a project that fits your availability, your interests, and your experience.

Why Volunteer

What You'll Gain

Volunteering with LGBT Voice Tanzania is an opportunity to grow, connect, and create lasting impact.

Be Part of a Movement

Become part of the ever-growing movement of LGBTQ+ people and allies working to build equity across communities in Tanzania and beyond.

Grow Your Network

Connect with pro-equality minded people and their professional and social networks. Build meaningful relationships with advocates and leaders.

Build Real Skills

Enhance your abilities and channel your creativity while strengthening our community — from fundraising and PR to event management and legal advocacy.

Nonprofit Experience

Gain hands-on experience in a human rights organisation. Strengthen your CV with meaningful, values-led work in the third sector.

Make New Friends

Meet people who share your values and your drive for change. Our community is warm, diverse, and genuinely welcoming to everyone.

Support LGBT+ Equality

Channel your professional skills — public relations, fundraising, legal, tech — directly into the most meaningful cause: equal rights for all.

Our Commitment

Equal Opportunity Policy

LGBT Voice Tanzania is committed to providing equal opportunity to all volunteers and applicants without regard to race, colour, religious creed, sex, gender identity, age, national origin, ancestry, citizenship status, physical or mental disability, medical condition, pregnancy, marital or veteran status, sexual orientation, or other personal characteristics as may be protected by applicable law.

This policy applies to all terms and conditions of engagement — including placement, promotion, transfer, leaves of absence, and compensation — and governs our relationships with outside vendors, customers, and the general public.
