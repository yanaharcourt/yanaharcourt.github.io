Who We Are

Our Mission

LGBT Voice Tanzania is a registered national organisation advancing equality, diversity, education, and justice for the LGBT+ community.

Advancing Human Rights

LGBT VOICE is a registered National Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual and Transgender (LGBT) organisation working to advance equality, diversity, education and justice. We work to advance recognition of human rights based on sexual orientation and gender identity at the national level — promoting clear national norms and mobilising international pressure where our government has failed to live up to those standards.

Building a National Movement

We are working to create a national network ensuring that groups and individuals working on these issues do so not in isolation, but as part of an effective, coordinated national movement — strengthening mechanisms for monitoring, documenting, and reporting human rights violations.

2013

Est. in Tanzania

Over a decade of uninterrupted advocacy for LGBT+ human rights.

National Network

Creating coordination between individuals and organisations working on LGBT+ issues across Tanzania.

100% Free Services

Crisis support, legal referrals, and community services — all provided at no cost to those in need.

Research

Our Published Study

Lived Realities of LGBTQ+ People in Tanzania

We are honoured to share our landmark report capturing the voices of 367 LGBTQ+ individuals across Tanzania. The report sheds light on their day-to-day experiences in education, healthcare, employment, housing, safety, and mental health — offering an evidence base for advocacy, policy, and press coverage.
