01
Ongoing

Ensuring Equal Rights of LGBTQ+ People in Tanzania

Our central advocacy initiative is dedicated to protecting and promoting the rights of LGBTQ+ individuals in a country where same-sex relationships are criminalised. We work to change perceptions through awareness campaigns, community dialogue, and international partnerships.

By engaging local leaders and educators, we foster broader understanding and acceptance — building sustainable strategies for long-term improvement.

Awareness campaigns reaching national audiences
Collaboration with international human-rights advocates
Community-led dialogues on inclusion and dignity

02
Concluding 2027

Addressing the Dynamic Needs of LGBTQ+ People

This ambitious five-year initiative meets the changing needs of LGBTQ+ individuals in Tanzania by combining statistical analysis with personal narratives — ensuring voices are heard and receive the recognition they deserve.

Through educational campaigns, community workshops, and partnerships with local organisations, we dismantle bias and cultivate an inclusive environment where everyone can thrive.

Data analysis on workplace discrimination and healthcare access
Personal narratives humanising statistics and fostering empathy
Proactive strategies to benefit future generations
Read the Lived Realities report
By examining both statistical data and personal stories, we aim to nurture an atmosphere of tolerance, foster mutual respect, and advance equality among diverse societal groups.

03
Emergency Response

Usalama House — LGBTQ+ Safe Space

A critical emergency initiative creating a nurturing sanctuary for LGBTQ+ youth facing displacement, intimidation, or family rejection due to their sexual orientation or gender identity.

Beyond temporary accommodation, Usalama House provides therapy, social integration support, and a community of care — empowering young people to build a more hopeful future.

Emergency temporary accommodation for displaced youth
Mental health therapy and trauma processing
Social integration and long-term community building
Get help or refer someone
The name means Usalama Safety in Swahili — a promise of protection to every young person who walks through our door.
24/7 Crisis support and safe access

Pink Heart Project

An innovative initiative committed to expanding educational opportunities for LGBTQ+ youth. In 2025, Pink Heart aims to award scholarships to 30 deserving young people — providing transformative experiences that can alter the course of their lives.

From resume workshops and interview preparation to networking events, Pink Heart equips participants with academic and career tools that open doors to fulfilling futures.
