How We Can Help

Situations We Support

LGBT Voice can help you with all of these situations — confidentially, and entirely on your terms.

Hate Crime

A hate crime can include verbal or written abuse, harassment, or physical violence. We can give you a space to talk. We can negotiate with your landlord, employer, or family members, or find you legal advice. If you want the police to know, we can make a report for you and liaise with them on your behalf — or you can make a report anonymously.

Abuse At Home

LGBT Voice is a safe space to talk and think about what you want, away from pressure at home. We can help you explore your options, plan to make you safer, look for safe housing, and speak with the police on your behalf.

Sexual Abuse & Violence

We help people who have been forced into sex, didn't agree to what happened, or are unsure how they feel about it — whether from a stranger or someone they know well. We won't pressure you, and will follow your lead entirely.

Problems With the Police

The police are there to keep law and order, but things don't always go the way they should. We can give advice and options, or make a formal complaint on your behalf — never pressuring you into anything you aren't comfortable with.

Our Promise

We can fight your corner.

We'll help you get what you need or record what happened. We will advocate for you, meaning we'll act on your behalf and try our hardest to ensure the outcome you need. We can also advocate for you by obtaining help from other organisations. We will never share your information without your consent, and we will never pressure you into taking action you aren't comfortable with. Wezesha is here for you.

Your Safety First

What You Can Expect

Confidential

Your information stays with us. We never share it without your explicit consent.

You're In Control

We follow your lead. No action is taken without your full agreement first.

Free of Charge

Every service we provide — advice, advocacy, and support — is completely free.
