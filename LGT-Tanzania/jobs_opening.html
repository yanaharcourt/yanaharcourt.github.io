About the Role

We are seeking a creative and committed Volunteer Social Media Manager to help amplify our message, grow our digital presence, and engage audiences with meaningful content. This is a unique opportunity to support LGBTQ+ rights in Tanzania by helping us tell our story and mobilise support locally and internationally.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and implement a consistent social media strategy across platforms (Facebook, Bluesky, Twitter/X, LinkedIn)
  • Create and schedule engaging, mission-aligned content — graphics, captions, reels, stories, infographics
  • Monitor and respond to messages, comments, and mentions in a timely manner
  • Track engagement metrics and suggest improvements to grow audience reach
  • Collaborate with staff to amplify events, campaigns, and advocacy messages
  • Ensure online safety and maintain confidentiality on sensitive topics

Qualifications

  • Strong understanding of social media platforms and trends
  • Excellent written communication skills in English
  • Graphic design skills — Canva, Adobe Spark, or similar
  • Commitment to LGBTQ+ rights, inclusion, and equality
  • Ability to work independently and manage time effectively
  • Experience in social media management or digital activism (preferred, not required)

What We Offer

Opportunity to contribute to a vital human rights cause
Professional experience in digital communications
Flexibility in work hours and location
A supportive and passionate team
Reference letter and recognition for your service
