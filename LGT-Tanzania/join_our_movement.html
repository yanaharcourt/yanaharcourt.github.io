Who We Are

A Community of Change-Makers

Standing Together for Justice

LGBT Voice Tanzania is an organisation of over 200 members based all across Tanzania. We're taking action to challenge the discrimination that LGBT+ people face every single day — in healthcare, employment, education, and housing.

If you want to step up and speak out against hate, our movement is the place for you. Whether you're an ally, an activist, or someone who simply believes in human dignity — there is a role for you here.

Join our network and we'll keep you informed when there is an LGBT+ case that needs your support, a campaign ready to launch, or a community that needs to hear your voice.
