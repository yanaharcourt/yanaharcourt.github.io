Empowering
BIPOC Communities

ASCENDtials is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit offering holistic educational tools, resources, and training programs aimed at uplifting San Diego's BIPOC community.

Our Impact

Where We Make a Difference

01
Environment

ASCENDtials has adopted nine highways and actively restores several beaches — working alongside CalTrans, Sweetwater Park, and partner organizations to clean up the world.

02
Mindfulness

Holistic wellness practices rooted in Kemetic Yoga and mindful living for BIPOC communities.

03
Education

Literacy workshops, civic engagement, and our Designing from Whiteness to Oneness™ curriculum.

04
BIPOC

Multiple programs uniting people of all backgrounds. Our Farmers' Markets celebrate diverse cultures and give BIPOC businesses the opportunity to connect and thrive within their communities.

Learn & Grow

Learning as a Tool
for Liberation

At ASCENDtials, we recognize that the current education system fails to equip individuals with the critical thinking and problem-solving skills needed to overcome systemic issues of racism and inequality. Our unique curriculum, Designing from Whiteness to Oneness™, shifts the focus toward empowering Black communities through dialogue and discussion.
