Youth Programs · Ages 3–17

Mini Earthkeepers,
growing tomorrow's stewards.

Mini ASCENDkemet Earthkeepers Teach Us (MAET) transforms our certified farmers' markets and waterfront parks into early learning ecosystems for BIPOC youth — where children explore nature and science through activities that protect and preserve the earth and humankind, powered by community.

What Youth Experience

Hands-on learning where
the classroom is outside

Each session blends ecological science with ancestral wisdom — giving BIPOC youth ages 3–17 a space to explore nature, build identity, and become stewards of their communities.

Garden-Based Learning

Ecological science exploring energy, materials, life, and change through hands in the soil. Children connect with the earth's cycles through direct cultivation experience.

Astronomy & Night Sky

Eyes on the stars — connecting cosmic cycles to ecological and cultural knowledge. Children explore their place in the universe through ancestral sky traditions.

Kemetic Yoga

Movement and mindfulness rooted in ancestral practice and cultural identity. Building physical awareness alongside deep cultural connection and inner resilience.

Cultural Storytelling

Ancestral stories and traditional drumming that connect youth to where they come from — bridging heritage with present-day identity and belonging.

Beach Cleanups & Water Science

Stewardship in action while learning the science of our coasts and waterways. Youth become active environmental guardians of San Diego's natural spaces.

Cultural Food Education

Connecting ancestral foods to nutrition, community, and the markets youth help run. From seed to market stall, food becomes a lens for culture and community.

Signature Feature · Ages 10–17

Grow it. Make it.
Bring it to market.

Youth ages 10–17 who complete a full program cycle become Youth Vendors at our certified farmers' markets — growing produce, crafting goods, and sharing their earthkeeping journey with the public.

  • Public speaking — practice presenting their work to real customers.
  • Financial literacy — handling sales, pricing, and earnings under staff supervision.
  • Community building — connecting with neighbors and sharing their earthkeeping story.

For Schools & Educators

Partner with MAET

MAET is actively building school partnerships through California's Farm to School program. Aligned with CDFA and CDE pillars, we help San Diego schools bring culturally rooted environmental education to their students — at whatever level fits your classroom.

Start small or go all in — every level is a step toward earthkeeping education. Four partnership pathways are available to schools at any stage.

Partner with Us
A

Market Field Trip

Bring your class to a certified farmers' market for a hands-on learning visit.

B

In-School Session

We come to you — a MAET-led session right inside your classroom.

C

Full Program Partnership

A monthly cycle with curriculum integration and pre/post assessments.

D

School Taste Test

Youth-grown produce taste tests connecting food, culture, and nutrition.

Night Sky Youth Camp

Stargazing at
Oak Oasis Park

As part of the MAET program, we offer separate Night Sky Sessions for Youth during night sky viewing gatherings at Oak Oasis Park. Evening sessions extend the same curriculum through astronomy-based learning and reflection.

Children gain another opportunity to connect with the universe, cultural wisdom, and their role as young earthkeepers and stewards of the earth.

Build My Spirit

Before & after school,
body and soul

Build My Spirit is a before and after school program for low-income BIPOC youth ages 5–17 in San Diego. The program provides children with activities that support them spiritually, mentally, and physically.

The key to all this is empowering the disadvantaged, and dismantling the unseen barriers of inequality — giving every child a foundation to grow from.
