Category 01

Literacy Workshops

Our Literacy Workshops restore ancestral knowledge and deepen participants' understanding of land, water, air, and cultural truth. These workshops are experiential, youth-friendly, and rooted in environmental justice.

Category 02

Workforce Development

Our Workforce Development programs prepare youth and adults for real-world roles in publishing, creative economy, environmental stewardship, and community enterprise. Participants gain hands-on experience, mentorship, and portfolio-building opportunities.
