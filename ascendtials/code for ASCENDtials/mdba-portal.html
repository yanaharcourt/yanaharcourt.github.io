About the MDBA Board

Connecting Leaders to the Tools They Need

Transparent governance, clear communication, and the onboarding of leaders committed to stewardship, culture, and community care.

The MDBA Board Portal connects current and prospective board members with the tools, information, and resources needed to guide the Marina District's future.

The Marina District Business Association is forming its founding board and inviting community-minded leaders, partners, and residents to help shape the future of our waterfront district.

If you're passionate about cultural programming, small-business support, community stewardship, and activating the Marina District with purpose and creativity, we'd love to have you at the table.

Clear Communication
Structured channels for board members to stay informed and aligned.
Governance
Transparent policies and accountability at every level.
Onboarding Support

Maritime & Safety

Supports waterfront safety, collaborates with harbor partners, and helps ensure safe, accessible community programming along the marina.

Community & Vendor Relations

Builds strong relationships with residents, vendors, and small businesses; supports outreach, communication, and community-centered engagement.

Culture

Creative & Experience

Shapes the cultural identity of the district through arts, wellness, and event programming, including our Farmers' markets and monthly Kettner Block Parties.

Operations

Operations Manager

Oversees logistics, scheduling, and coordination across MDBA programs to ensure smooth, efficient, and well-supported community experiences.
