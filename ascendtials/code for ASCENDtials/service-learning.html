Our Mission

What do
we do?

We are creating opportunities for our youth. They will gain skills that make them more aware of society and nature. We will also equip them to be competitive in the job market. We want them to be self-starters who solve problems, not create them.

Students can choose from sessions that explore various aspects of nature — Earth, Fire, Water, and Air. All four factors necessitate deeper investigation and comprehension. Our programs attempt to take into account a comprehensive view of education across all four elements. Sessions are totally customizable based on the learner.

Program Goals
What we're working toward
  • Revitalize communities with BIPOC-focused workshops.
  • Emphasize balance, harmony, justice, order, and reciprocity.
  • Raise awareness about farming's impact on soil and water.
  • Promote holistic environmental practices.
Program Benefits
What you will gain
  • Engages students in nature-centered service learning.
  • Develops critical thinking, leadership, and environmental stewardship values.
  • Exposes students to diverse environmental career paths.
  • Raises awareness of community needs and enhances interpersonal skills.
  • Enhances resumes, scholarship applications, and networking opportunities.

How It Works

Explore our
learning programs

Students can choose from sessions that explore various aspects of nature. There are activities for the earth, fire, water, and air. All of these factors necessitate a deeper investigation and comprehension. Our programs attempt to entice students by seeking a comprehensive view of education and taking into account all four factors.

Students can make a difference by focusing on one or more areas. Sessions are totally customizable based on the learner. We can supply 15–25 hours per academic term, with a weekly demand of 3–4 hours.

To learn more, join us here or contact us at info@ascendtials.org, and someone from our staff will get back to you. Please include experiential/service learning in the subject line.
