Our Mission

Thoughtful tools for
lasting change

We seek Oneness that builds capacity and environmental care — ensuring that service is both restorative and transformative.

ASCENDtials provides thoughtful, holistic, and educative tools, economic initiatives, and collaborative partnerships that use principles and practical activities to advance cultural wisdom, food equity, and sustainable community development.

Through our expanded role as a Maintenance Assessment District administrator, we support local businesses, enhance public spaces, and foster inclusive economic growth that reflects the cultural integrity and wellness of the communities we serve.

By blending wellness, volunteerism, and environmental stewardship, ASCENDtials empowers individuals and communities to thrive while building scalable models that can be replicated across states.

Truth & Transformation

Guided by the
Seven Principles of Maat

African philosophies and principles are at our core. We turn to the wisdom of our ancestors from Kemet — the indigenous name given to ancient Egypt — to guide us. They founded a system that not only imitates nature but provides a deeper understanding of our Oneness, true nature, and unity with all things in existence. It is through their teachings that we will find truth and transformation.

Truth
Ma'at
Speaking and living in truth as the foundation of all relationships.
Justice
Netjer
Upholding fairness and accountability in every community relationship and structure.
Harmony
Hetep
Creating peace within ourselves and with the world around us.
Balance
Saa
Maintaining equilibrium between giving and receiving, action and rest, self and community.
Order
Neheh
Living with intention and structure that honors natural cycles.
Reciprocity
Iru
What we give to community, community returns to us tenfold.
Oneness
Amen
Recognizing our unity with all living things and the cosmos — the principle that binds all others together.
