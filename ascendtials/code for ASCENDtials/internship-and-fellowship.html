How to Apply

Four steps to
begin your journey

Our internship program welcomes students from all majors ready to explore a holistic, nature-centered approach to learning. The process is straightforward — explore tracks, submit your interest, and we take it from there.

01
Review Internship Tracks

Explore available pathways to understand roles, expectations, and skills you'll develop. Each track supports our mission in environmental justice, community education, and organizational development.

02
Submit Interest Form

Complete the online form so we can learn about your background, interests, and availability. This step places you into our review queue for consideration.

03
Await Initial Review

Our team reviews submissions weekly. Eligible applicants are invited to attend interviews to discuss the role, expectations, and team alignment.

04
Create Your Dashboard

If selected, you'll receive instructions to set up your ASCENDtials Dashboard — your central hub for signing your agreement, accessing onboarding materials, and program updates.

Common Questions

Internship
FAQs

Everything you need to know before applying. Can't find your answer? Reach out to us directly.

Contact Us
What are my hours?
You're expected to be available Monday–Friday between 11:00 a.m. and 2:00 p.m. PST. Work is a combination of online and in-person workshops. If your internship is 10 hours per week, a minimum of 3 hours per month will be in workshops. All hours must be documented weekly through the timekeeping app.
Are these paid positions?
These aren't paid positions. Students are able to receive academic credit, letters of reference, and valuable real-world skills and tools that last a lifetime.
What is the expected workload?
Most interns work up to 20 hours per week and are available from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. PDT. Internships follow a syllabus with expected outcomes, regular check-ins with mentors, and presentations/learning sessions with deliverables.
How long is the internship period?
Internships follow school terms and are three to six months in length. They end in June or after the semester ends.
Who do I contact when I need time off?
Notify your supervisor as soon as possible — ideally a few days in advance. If unavoidable with one day's notice, please secure your own substitute and inform your supervisor of the change.
Do we have any major requirements or language prerequisites?
We allow students to apply based on interest and experience in the role. Each role specifies its own requirements. We require a resume and proof of enrollment from all applicants.
What is the social media policy?
Please don't spend time on social media during work hours, and refrain from posting about your internship or our organization on social media without prior approval.
What happens when my internship ends?
You'll complete an evaluation process with your lead and hiring manager. Once complete, you're encouraged to apply for any open positions. ASCENDtials considers all former interns and volunteers part of our Alumni community.
How often should I check in with my supervisor?
At least once a week. On your first day, you'll be given a recurring meeting time with your team lead and the hiring manager.
What if I'm overwhelmed?
Talk to your mentor to ensure you understand project expectations correctly, or speak with your lead to reevaluate expectations and your workload. Everyone here wants you to succeed.
