Your Volunteer Journey

Getting started
is simple

We've designed a simple, supportive onboarding process to help you get started with confidence. Here's what to expect.

1
Explore Volunteer Pathways
Choose the role that aligns with your interests, skills, and availability. Review the four pathways above and decide whether you'd like to volunteer in-person or remotely.
2
Select Your Volunteer Membership
ASCENDtials uses a one-time $25 membership donation model to sustain the tools and resources that make your volunteer experience possible. A waiver option is available — no explanation required. Remote volunteers complete this before onboarding; in-person volunteers during onboarding.
3
Create Your Volunteer Portal Account
Your portal gives you access to training, communication tools, hour tracking, program materials, appreciation letters, and a living checklist that orients you to our tools and team.
4
Attend Onboarding (40 Minutes)
We meet every Monday and Thursday for a brief orientation. This 40-minute session introduces you to our mission, programs, expectations, tools, and next steps. All volunteers complete onboarding before beginning their pathway.

Membership Levels

Find the right
membership for you

Every volunteer and participant joins under a membership type that determines portal access and available features.

Type Access Features
Remote Teammate Volunteer Portal Dashboard, hour tracking, training tools, remote operations access
In-Person Volunteer Volunteer Portal Dashboard, hour tracking, training tools, event sign-up
Single Participant Membership Portal Calendar, uploads, events, workshops, stewardship activities, and learning sessions
Family Participant Membership Portal Up to 4 linked accounts with shared portal access
Court-Ordered Volunteer Portal Required hours tracking, proof uploads, auto-generated completion letters
Company Participant Membership Portal Group access, team tracking, and company community service documentation

Monthly Presentation Requirement: Each team delivers a monthly presentation summarizing their progress, contributions, and learning over the previous four weeks. Teams share key accomplishments, challenges, data insights, and one story or observation that illustrates the real-world value of their efforts — strengthening cross-team understanding and connecting individual roles to ASCENDtials' broader mission.

Accessibility & Waivers

Community service for everyone

We believe volunteering should remain accessible to all, regardless of financial circumstances. No one is turned away from serving their community.

Donation Waivers Available If you experience financial hardship, request a waiver after onboarding. No explanation is required — your dignity and access come first.
A Shared Investment Your membership donation is not a fee — it's a shared commitment to sustaining the work we do together. It helps maintain technology, training coordination, and keeps programs free for families.
Built on Shared Care Your service helps create healing, learning, and empowerment for families across San Diego. We're grateful you're here.

Portal Features

Everything in
one place

Your volunteer portal is built to make service easy — from the moment you sign up to the day you download your completion letter. All managed automatically, so you can focus on what matters.

Dashboard Home
Welcome screen with quick links, announcements, orientation checklist, and a living overview of your progress and upcoming activities.
Activity Calendar
Sign up directly for cleanups, farmers' markets, workshops, and community events. See what's upcoming and reserve your spot.
Upload Proof
Submit photos, PDFs, and confirmations directly in the portal. Court-ordered volunteers can upload all required documentation here.

Track hours, activities, and completion milestones. See your volunteer journey visualized and stay motivated as you grow.
Letters & Documents
Download auto-generated appreciation letters and certificates when requirements are met. Court-ordered letters issued automatically at completion.
