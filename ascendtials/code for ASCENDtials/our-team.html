Our Volunteers

Driven by Passion,
Powered by
Community

Ascendtials operates on a passionate and talented group of volunteers who help bring our mission to existence. Whether supporting us by volunteering in person at one of our many events or remotely from around the world, our volunteers dedicate their time and skills to help ASCENDtials spread its message of building communities, protecting the environment, and more.

Our Teams of Volunteers

Business Development

Work with our partners and donors to support programs and develop activities that align with our mission.

Graphics Design

Create all of our stunning art used in fliers and monthly campaigns that promote our programs.

Web Development

Keep our website running, creating new pages and updating existing ones with the latest information.

Publishing

Write and revise all of the written content for our various posters and marketing materials.

Hiring

Support the recruitment and onboarding process of all of our talented volunteers across all teams.
