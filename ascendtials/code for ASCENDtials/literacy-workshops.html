Civic Literacy

Our civic workshops include discussions on our Whiteness to Oneness™ Curriculum alongside healing sessions that explore cultural elements. We use the Design Thinking process and the ancient Dogon creation process to bridge the gap between policy and people — creating informed, empowered advocates.

  • Whiteness to Oneness™ Curriculum discussions
  • Cultural healing sessions
  • Design Thinking process
  • Ancient Dogon creation process
  • Voting rights & civic engagement
  • Know your rights — legal literacy
  • Advocacy & community organizing
  • Local government & policy

Environmental Literacy

As the climate warms and car exhaust pollutes our streets, we are learning that trees are more important than ever. Join us in our tree planting initiative at the ASCENDkemet Farmers' Market with our partners at Tree San Diego to help plant trees all across San Diego.

Join us in helping organize, set-up, and clean-up in places around our beautiful community. Sign up for any available session to aid us in our mission to create cleaner and safer environments.

  • Tree planting with Tree San Diego
  • ASCENDkemet Farmers' Market partnership
  • Community clean-up & organizing events
  • Air quality & environmental justice advocacy
  • Land stewardship and urban ecology
  • Climate resilience in BIPOC neighborhoods

Financial Literacy

Make informed decisions about your money. Our Generational Wealth Workshops offer a comprehensive approach to building and maintaining generational wealth, with a focus on land preservation and addressing trans-generational trauma.

  • Generational Wealth Workshop series
  • Land preservation strategies
  • Addressing trans-generational financial trauma
  • Budgeting & personal finance fundamentals
  • Credit building & debt management
  • Black entrepreneurship & business ownership
  • Investing & wealth-building strategies

Cosmic Literacy

Our workshops aim to deepen knowledge of ancient astronomy and astrology. They include a new night sky course, evening Kemetic Yoga, and basic lessons on the constellations, cycles, life stages, and the moon.

  • Ancient astronomy & astrology
  • New night sky course
  • Evening Kemetic Yoga sessions
  • Constellations, cycles, and life stages
  • Moon phases & celestial rhythms
  • Night Sky Star Party — every 2nd Saturday
  • Guided telescope viewing for families
  • 21-day immersive course available

Our workshops offer a grounded space for the BIPOC community to exchange experiences, reconnect with ancestral knowledge, and work collectively toward systemic change. Each session is experiential, youth-friendly, and rooted in environmental and social justice.

Our Approach

Rooted in culture,
built for healing

Our workshops go beyond information transfer. They are living spaces — grounded in the reality of BIPOC experience and oriented toward collective liberation. Every session is designed to be accessible, affirming, and transformative.

Community-centered Every workshop is co-created with and for the BIPOC communities we serve.
Experiential & hands-on Learning that moves beyond lectures — into dialogue, practice, and shared experience.
