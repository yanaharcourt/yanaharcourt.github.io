Why Partner With Us

Your support drives
meaningful change

ASCENDtials delivers tangible programs and initiatives, giving sponsors clear evidence of the positive outcomes their support makes possible. Partnerships connect you directly with diverse communities, amplifying visibility and demonstrating authentic commitment to equity and sustainability.

Direct Community Access
Connect directly with San Diego's diverse BIPOC communities. Your brand appears at our Farmers' Markets, food distributions, educational workshops, and community events.
Measurable Impact
We provide transparent reporting on how your sponsorship dollars are deployed — from educational outcomes to the number of families served each month at our food distributions.
Authentic Equity Commitment
Align your organization with a mission dedicated to advancing environmental justice, cultural awareness, and education — and demonstrate that commitment authentically through action, not just statements.
Brand Visibility Across Platforms
Sponsors receive recognition across our website, social media channels (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube), event signage, and email newsletters reaching thousands of engaged community members throughout San Diego.
Fueling Possibilities
Your sponsorship fuels more than programs — it fuels possibilities. Every contribution directly supports initiatives that empower individuals and create lasting change in our neighborhoods.

Our Alumni

A community of
former contributors

ASCENDtials considers all former employees and volunteers to be Alumni of the organization. We hope to continue building these relationships with community leaders, supporting them with access to our resources, and connecting them with our members and financial supporters.

Former Employees
Staff who shaped our programs and operations over the years — they remain part of the ASCENDtials family.
Former Volunteers

How It Works

Start your
partnership today

Feel empowered and advocate for your community. Here's how to begin a partnership with ASCENDtials.

1
Reach Out
Contact us via email or our contact form to express your interest. Tell us about your organization and what kind of partnership you have in mind.
2
Explore Fit
We'll set up a conversation to align on goals, programs, and impact areas where your support can make the greatest difference.
3
Formalize the Partnership
We'll put together an agreement that outlines commitments, recognition, and reporting so both sides know what to expect.
4
Create Change Together
Your support goes directly into programs. We'll keep you updated with impact reports and invite you to see your contribution in action at events.
