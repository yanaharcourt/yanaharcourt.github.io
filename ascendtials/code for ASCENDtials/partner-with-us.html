How We Work Together

Partnership opportunities

We collaborate with community organizations, companies, civic entities, and social justice groups through an approach built on co-creation and shared purpose.

Corporate Sponsorship

Support our farmers' markets, food distributions, youth programs, and education initiatives. In return, gain visibility with our growing community, co-branding opportunities, and a chance to demonstrate authentic community investment in San Diego's BIPOC neighborhoods.

Our Community

Real impact, real people

Every partnership directly serves our neighbors — from food-insecure families in City Heights to youth seeking mentorship and environmental advocates protecting our shared land.

Civic & Institutional

We partner with government agencies, schools, and public institutions — like our existing collaborations with the City of San Diego (Adopt-A-Highway) and I Love A Clean San Diego (Adopt-A-Beach).

Social Justice Organizations

We join forces with groups aligned with our mission — co-hosting events, sharing resources, and amplifying each other's work in education, environmental justice, and community wellness.

Why Partner

Why partner with
ASCENDtials?

We build relationships grounded in shared purpose, transparent accountability, and real community impact in Southern California.

  • Community-centered programming
  • Environmental and cultural education
  • Youth empowerment
  • Strong operational systems
  • Grant-ready documentation
  • Clear agreements and expectations
  • Impact tracking and reporting
Partnership Agreement Requirement

All ASCENDtials partnerships require a signed Partnership Agreement. This ensures clarity, alignment, and accountability for both parties and supports accurate reporting for grants and impact measurement.

How It Works

1

We track participation and impact for all partners.

Questions?

Partnership FAQs

Everything you need to know about working with ASCENDtials. Can't find your answer? Write to us at info@ascendtials.org.

What kinds of organizations do you partner with?
We partner with a wide range of organizations — community nonprofits, corporations, civic entities (like city agencies), schools, social justice groups, and individual professionals. If your values align with critical consciousness, community empowerment, and environmental care, we'd love to talk.
How does a corporate sponsorship work?
Corporate sponsors typically support specific programs or events — such as our farmers' markets, food distributions, or youth programs. In return, sponsors receive co-branding, recognition in our communications, and the opportunity to participate directly in community events. Reach out to discuss a package tailored to your goals.
Are donations to ASCENDtials tax-deductible?
Yes. ASCENDtials is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN 47-2451577), so all qualifying contributions are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law. We are a Candid Platinum-rated organization.
What is the Alumni Program?
ASCENDtials considers all former employees and volunteers to be lifelong Alumni. Alumni receive priority event access, our newsletter, gifts, gear discounts, and continued access to our resource network. We believe in maintaining these relationships with community leaders long after their formal role has ended.
How do we get started?
Simply reach out via email at info@ascendtials.org or use our Contact page. We'll schedule a conversation to learn about your organization, discuss mutual goals, and explore the best partnership structure for your needs. We look forward to hearing from you.
