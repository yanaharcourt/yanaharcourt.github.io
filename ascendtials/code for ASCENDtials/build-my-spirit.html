Our Mission

Empowering youth to rise

The Build My Spirit program is the cornerstone of our organization. We believe in empowering young people by fostering open dialogue around relevant issues. Our science-based education approach is fundamental to self-discovery and purpose.

Build My Spirit provides youth with knowledge about nature, their place in society, wellness tools, coping strategies, creative thinking, problem-solving, and overall quality of life. We use old wisdom and modern ideas to create programs that keep youth healthy, self-aware, and connected to their communities.

We prioritize instilling strong values and boundaries to protect our youth from life's challenges — equipping them with the knowledge and skills to thrive in existing systems while working toward dismantling inequalities.

MAET · Mini ASCENDkemet Earthkeepers Teach Us

What youth experience

MAET transforms our certified farmers' markets and waterfront parks into early learning ecosystems for BIPOC youth ages 3–17 — where children explore nature and science through activities that protect and preserve the earth and humankind, powered by community.

Garden-Based Learning
Ecological science exploring energy, materials, life, and change through hands in the soil. Youth grow, tend, and harvest — building responsibility and wonder alongside science literacy.
🧘🏾
Kemetic Yoga
Movement and mindfulness rooted in ancestral practice and cultural identity.
📖
Cultural Storytelling
Ancestral stories and traditional drumming connecting youth to where they come from.
🌊
Beach Cleanups & Water Science
Stewardship in action while learning the science of our coasts and waterways.
✨
Astronomy & Night Sky
Eyes on the stars — connecting cosmic cycles to ecological and cultural knowledge.
🥕
Cultural Food Education
Connecting ancestral foods to nutrition, community, and the markets youth help run.

Signature Feature · Ages 10–17

Youth Vendor
Pathway

Youth ages 10–17 who complete a full program cycle become Youth Vendors at our certified farmers' markets — growing produce, crafting goods, and sharing their earthkeeping journey with the public.

Under staff supervision, they practice public speaking, financial literacy, and community building. Grow it, make it, bring it to market.

Night Sky Sessions

Night Sky Youth Camp at Oak Oasis Park

As part of our Build My Spirit framework, we offer Night Sky Sessions for youth during night sky viewing gatherings at Oak Oasis Park — extending the curriculum through astronomy-based learning and reflection.

These evening sessions give children another opportunity to connect with the universe, cultural wisdom, and their role as young earthkeepers and stewards. An Upcoming Overnight Camp program is coming soon!

For Schools & Educators

Partner with MAET

MAET is actively building school partnerships through California's Farm to School program. Aligned with CDFA and CDE pillars, we help San Diego schools bring culturally rooted environmental education to their students — at whatever level fits your classroom. Start small or go all in.

A

Market Field Trip

Bring your class to a certified farmers' market for a hands-on learning visit. A perfect introduction to our ecosystem.

B

In-School Session

We come to you — a MAET-led session right inside your classroom. No travel required for students.

C

Full Program Partnership

A monthly cycle with curriculum integration, pre/post assessments, and a dedicated MAET educator.

D

School Taste Test

Youth-grown produce taste tests connecting food, culture, and nutrition — right in the cafeteria.
