The Curriculum

A transition from
separation to solidarity

"It signifies a transition from a culture built around white privilege to one that equally recognizes the creative energy, transformative power, and skill of Black bodies."

Designing From Whiteness to Oneness™ is a curriculum helping learners critically and consciously engage in dialogue through presentations, storytelling, and sensory awareness. Using KOS (Knowledge of Self) tools and frameworks, it creates a dialogical process of learning that motivates transformation.

Our goal is to empower Black communities to solve problems through deep discovery and discussion of Anti-Black structures — exploring the oppressor and oppressed model, and how it continually dehumanizes communities without sufficient support or a seat at the table.

Defining the journey
What is Whiteness?
Whiteness represents the anti-thesis caste system we find ourselves in — a structure of imposed hierarchy, colonialist imposition, and the Anti-Black dialogue that has prompted civil rights movements. The oppressed internalize their position and, through historical structures, are made to strive toward becoming oppressors — creating an unending cycle only Blacks can lead with proper support.
The destination
What is Oneness?
Oneness can be demonstrated by those in solidarity with highly melanated Black bodies, but must come from the oppressed themselves. True generosity lies in striving so that hands need to be extended less in supplication — so that more and more they become human hands which work and, working, transform the world.
Topics We Explore

Building critical
consciousness

These materials are the foundation of the organization. Each topic is woven into all programming — from healing sessions to yoga, environmental programs, and community organizing.

01
Knowledge of Self & Earth
Deep exploration of ancestral identity, melanated heritage, and the relationship between Black bodies and the natural world.
02
Oppressor & Oppressed
Critical study of how oppressive systems operate, how they are internalized, and how both oppressor and oppressed can begin to break the cycle.
03
History & Culture
Understanding how things came to be — from colonial imposition to modern Anti-Black structures — so that communities can chart paths forward.
04
Socialization & Power
Examining the Cycle of Socialization and how political power is distributed, maintained, and challenged within communities.
05
Leadership & Accountability
Developing the influence, initiative, and accountability structures needed to hold leaders responsible and reinforce systemic change.
06
Rebuilding Solidarity
Dismantling structures of division and developing a new culture through community partnerships, youth engagement, and collective care.
Our Framework

The tools we
teach with

We teach this curriculum through workshops, retreats, yoga sessions, healing sessions, and environmental programs — incorporating KOS, Melanin-ology, and Ancestral knowledge as integrated learning tools.

KOS — Knowledge of Self
Our core framework providing the tools and vocabulary to attune to higher frequencies via ancient Ancestral applied knowledge. KOS guides participants to affect change in their own communities by instilling urgency, self-awareness, and a process of critical discovery.
I
KOS-Melanin-ology™
Science-informed exploration of Melanin's role in the human body, African heritage, and spiritual practice.
II
Storytelling & Dialogue
Structured storytelling, presentations, and sensory awareness exercises to open genuine dialogue around truth and identity.
III
Ancestral Knowledge
Preserving and applying ancient wisdom to present-day challenges — a complete human experience through historical and cultural grounding.
IV
Third Eye Development
Bringing greater awareness to the five senses, including developing the third eye, as a pathway to critical consciousness and spiritual clarity.

Who This Reaches

Built for
the whole community

The curriculum is designed to reach the parents, community leaders, and local businesses needed to construct viable partnerships that address community needs and develop the skills to improve social relations at home.

  • Parents & Families
    Building social competency that bridges the gap to protect children and prepare them with community opportunities.
  • Community Leaders
    Creating opportunities that cater to changes within the community and keep relationships in balance.
  • Educators & Scholars
    Youth, young people, and educators engaging in conversations that plant seeds of change from schools outward.
  • Local Businesses & Partners
    We establish partnerships with community leaders and businesses to better view needs, build capacity, and organize around shared values.
