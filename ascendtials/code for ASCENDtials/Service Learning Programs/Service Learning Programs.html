Our Experiential and Service Learning Program invites undergraduate and graduate college students to join our efforts in community engagement, social advocacy, and environmental justice as an intern. Move beyond the classroom and develop civic knowledge and practical skills while working for our nonprofit.

What do we do?

We are creating opportunities for our youth. They will gain skills that make them more aware of society and nature. We will also equip them to be competitive in the job market. We want them to be self-starters who solve problems, not create them.

Environmental Programs

Program Goals

  1. Revitalize communities with BIPOC-focused workshops.
  2. Emphasize balance, harmony, justice, order, and reciprocity.
  3. Raise awareness about farming's impact on soil and water.
  4. Promote holistic environmental practices.

Program Benefits

  1. Engages students in nature-centered service learning.
  2. Develops critical thinking, leadership, and environmental stewardship values.
  3. Exposes students to diverse environmental career paths.
  4. Raises awareness of community needs and enhances interpersonal skills.
  5. Enhances resumes, scholarship applications, and networking opportunities.

Service-Learning Programs

ASCENDtials' unique Service-Learning Program offers real-time Volunteer and Internship roles, providing students with opportunities for the practical application of knowledge that heals, crafting entrepreneurial and business acumen ready futures.

Gain hands-on experience as a legal aid for our nonprofit. We are dedicated to providing low-income BIPOC families with comprehensive legal support. You will be responsible for conducting research, drafting documents, and providing legal advice and counseling for the community.

Help us maintain our organization’s image. As a public relations intern, you will be responsible for assisting with media outreach, monitoring media coverage, and promoting our events. You will play a crucial role in gaining visibility for our organization.

Help out at our events! You will be vital to the efficient planning, organization, and implementation of our various programs. You will also be responsible for answering emails and fielding questions about our programs.

Identify and cultivate relationships with prospective individual, foundation, and corporate donors. You will also be responsible for coordinating fundraising activities to support ASCENDtials’ initiatives. Must be local to San Diego.

Help us organize and run our various events. Manage event details such as catering, entertainment, transportation, location, and equipment, and ensure everything runs smoothly. Must be local to San Diego.

Plan, create, and code our web pages. As a web designer intern, you will be responsible for reviewing website templates, refining our designs, experimenting with website features, and enhancing user experience.

Create and edit flyers, banners, event logos, and other graphics. Help us create compelling digital content and grow your portfolio. Stretch your design skills, foster creativity, and gain experience working with Canva.

Take charge of our internal and external newsletters. Create our monthly newsletters and ensure they are sent out in a timely manner. Help us grow our readership and improve the quality of our content.

Perform clerical and administrative duties essential to keeping our organization running smoothly. You will be responsible for answering emails, setting up meetings, calendar management, and all things customer service.

Edit all social media, PR, and website content. Proofread copy for grammatical, spelling, punctuation, and formatting errors, and improve readability. Follow our style guide to ensure all copy adheres to the organization's style and voice.

Crafting compelling copy for social media posts, newsletters, website content, and other marketing materials. Do necessary research on assigned topics. Write content that suits our organization’s style and tone.

Research and draft grant proposals and applications to acquire funding for our organization. Build relationships with current funders, and reach out to new potential partners. You will play a crucial role in keeping our organization running.

EXPLORE OUR LEARNING PROGRAMS

Students can choose from sessions that explore various aspects of nature. There are activities for the earth, fire, water, and air. All of these factors necessitate a deeper investigation and comprehension. Our programs attempt to entice students by seeking a comprehensive view of education and taking into account all four factors. Students can make a difference by focusing on one or more areas. Sessions are totally customizable based on the learner. We can supply 15-25 hours per academic term, with a weekly demand of 3-4 hours.
