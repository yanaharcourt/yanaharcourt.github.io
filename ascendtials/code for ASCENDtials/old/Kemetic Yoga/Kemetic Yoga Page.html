KEMETIC YOGA

Kemetic Yoga is an ancient yoga practice originating from the land of Kemet—a name given to Egypt by its ancient indigenous inhabitants. The practice connects breath and slow movement, guiding the body through poses inspired by the ancient illustrations of Egyptian gods and goddesses. It’s a spiritual practice, moving energy through the body to connect with our higher selves and our ancestors.

Why Do Kemetic Yoga?

Practicing Kemetic yoga connects us to a rich ancient African heritage largely forgotten. Our ancient African ancestors used Kemetic yoga to connect with themselves and higher beings. Divine movement brought them health, wealth, and harmony. Rediscovering this practice for ourselves will grant us that same wisdom. Challenge mainstream yoga culture and find your inner power.
