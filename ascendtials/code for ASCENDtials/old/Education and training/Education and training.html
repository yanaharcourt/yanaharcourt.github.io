At ASCENDtials, we believe the current education system sustains systemic racism and oppressive structures. We've worked to develop a teaching curriculum, for all ages, that offers alternative methods of thinking. We teach this curriculum through literacy workshops, retreats, yoga sessions, healing sessions and environmental programs.

ASCENDTIALS LITERACY WORKSHOPS

Purpose

These workshops offer a safe space for the BIPOC community to share experiences, heal, and work toward systemic change.

Topics

We offer workshops on the following topics:

Civic Literacy

Cosmic Literacy

Environmental Literacy

Spiritual Literacy

Financial Literacy

FOUNDATIONAL COURSEWORK

Designing From Whiteness to Oneness™ is the core of our curriculum. It is an exploration, understanding, and analysis of oppressive structures, while simultaneously developing new ways of thinking that work to challenge them. We want learners to critically, cosmically, and consciously engage in dialogue centered around Blackness.
