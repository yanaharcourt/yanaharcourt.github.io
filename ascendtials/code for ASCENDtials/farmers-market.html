Created by
and for the
BIPOC community.

The ASCENDKemet Collective Certified Farmers' Market exists to celebrate and represent the BIPOC (Black, Indigenous, and People of Color) community of San Diego through its local vendors, farmers, and artisans. Rooted in cultural pride and ecological stewardship, our market uplifts the highly melanated diasporic community while championing holistic living, bartering, and access to fresh, nutritious produce. Our open-air market is more than a place to shop — it is a vibrant hub where locally grown and harvested foods meet artistry, wellness, and reciprocity.

Available Programs

Eat well for less

Research some of the available programs — SNAP/EBT, WIC, and SFMNP — that you may be eligible for. It's worth the work; we accept these at our markets, so you may be able to save money!

SNAP / EBT

  • SNAP/EBT are most definitely accepted at our farmers' markets!
  • PSA: due to the ongoing Federal government shutdown, CalFresh (aka SNAP) benefits for the month of November will be delayed from being deposited onto your EBT card. For more information, check back for updates.
  • For information on food resources, please visit the 211 San Diego website.

WIC (Women, Infants & Children) Program

  • You can use your WIC Card to shop for fresh produce at select farmers' markets.
  • You can buy fresh and organic fruits & vegetables.
  • Learn more about shopping with WIC at the farmers' market here.
$26
per month for children ages 1 to 5
$47
per month for people who are pregnant and postpartum
$52
per month for people who are breastfeeding
$78
per month for individuals breastfeeding more than one infant

SFMNP (Senior Farmers' Market Nutrition) Program

  • Serves low-income seniors, ages 60+.
  • Provides fresh, locally grown fruits, vegetables, herbs, and honey.
  • Expands awareness and use of local farmers' markets & community programs.
  • Stipend checks are issued to participants annually for use from May 1st to November 30th. May only be redeemed at WIC-authorized farmers' markets.

Elder Stability Navigation

  • 20–30 minute diversion conversations.
  • Identifying safe alternatives to shelter.
  • Connecting elders to benefits, family, or temporary housing.
  • Providing small, flexible support (gift cards, bus passes, groceries).

Elder Resource & Benefits Clinic

  • Help elders apply for benefits (SSI, CalFresh, Medi-Cal, LIHEAP).
  • Connect them to rental assistance programs.
  • Provide transportation support.
  • Offer housing navigation referrals.

Stamp Cards

We're excited to introduce Stamp Cards, rewarding loyal shoppers! Customers earn a stamp with each purchase of $10 or more, and when they complete a side of the card, they receive $5 in market credit. This program encourages repeat visits, boosts vendor sales, and keeps our community engaged and coming back for more. Visit our farmers' market information booth to get yours today!
