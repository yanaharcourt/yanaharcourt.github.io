Our membership structure supports two different pathways to keep things accessible and clear — whether you're here to serve, or here to learn.

Service Pathway

Volunteering

Requires a Volunteer Membership — mandatory for all volunteers. Ideal for those who want to give their time in an open-ended way, contributing to events, markets, and community programs.

You Qualify If
This sounds like you
  • You're seeking structured learning, not just volunteer hours
  • You're a student, young adult, or community member wanting hands-on experience
  • You're exploring environmental, cultural, or wellness-based fields
  • You want to contribute to community events while learning
  • You prefer a guided, educational experience rather than open-ended volunteering
