Ancient Roots

A practice born
in Kemet

"Our ancient ancestors used these practices to connect with themselves and the divine — divine movement brought health, wealth, and harmony."

Kemetic Yoga is an ancient practice originating from the land of Kemet — modern-day Egypt — developed by its indigenous inhabitants thousands of years ago. Rooted in breath mastery and slow, intentional movement, it guides the body through poses inspired by the hieroglyphic illustrations found on the walls of temples and tombs.

This is more than exercise. It is a spiritual practice — moving energy through the body to heal, to connect with our higher selves, and to honor the wisdom of our ancestors. Rediscovering this practice reconnects us to a rich diasporic heritage that has been largely forgotten.

The Benefits

Why practice
Kemetic Yoga?

Practicing Kemetic Yoga connects us to a rich ancient diasporic heritage largely forgotten. The same wisdom that brought our ancestors health, wealth, and harmony is available to us today — through breath, movement, and intention.

01
Physical Health

Slow, precise movement builds flexibility, joint health, and postural alignment without strain — accessible to all bodies and fitness levels.

02
Mental Clarity

Breathwork and meditation reduce anxiety, sharpen focus, and cultivate the stillness needed to navigate daily life with intention.

03
Spiritual Connection

Reconnect with higher self and ancestral energy through the same poses depicted in ancient Kemetic temples and tombs.

04
Community Belonging

Each session is a gathering — a space where neighbors, families, and youth share in the collective practice of ancestral wellness.

Our Framework

Rooted in our curricula

Our Kemetic Yoga sessions are grounded in ASCENDtials' signature frameworks — Whiteness to Oneness™ and KOS-Melanin-ology™. We use movement as an entry point for deeper conversations about identity, systemic inequality, and the science of Melanin.

Whiteness to Oneness™
Our foundational curriculum dismantles structural inequality and fosters constructive dialogue. Yoga becomes a space to challenge oppressive structures while building new ways of thinking and being.
KOS-Melanin-ology™
A science-informed curriculum exploring the role of Melanin in the human body, African heritage, and spiritual practice — woven directly into movement and breathwork.
I
Ancestral Poses
Each posture is drawn from temple and tomb illustrations, recreating the exact forms held by our ancient ancestors.
II
Breathwork Mastery
Air quality and breath control are central. Participants develop a personal breathing practice that extends beyond the yoga mat.
III
Dialogue & Reflection
Sessions include structured discussion, creating space for honest conversation about identity, healing, and community wellness.
