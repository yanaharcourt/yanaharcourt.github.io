What We Offer

More than groceries —
a whole community event

Each distribution day brings together fresh food, sustainability, and wraparound support for families in City Heights and surrounding neighborhoods.

Fresh Groceries for Your Family

Register in advance to receive a bag of fresh groceries. Pick-up is available by vehicle or walk-up with confirmation. The majority of recipients are women-led households with four or more members.

2025 Year in Review

Thank you for an
incredible year

In 2025, our community came together in powerful ways. Thanks to your generosity, partnership, and volunteer spirit, we hosted six food distributions — each one providing essential support to our neighbors.

We also welcomed community resource vendors who showed up on the second Friday of each month at Jeremy Henwood Park to offer services, information, and care to families across City Heights. Your presence made these gatherings more than distributions — they became moments of connection, dignity, and shared resilience.

Looking ahead to 2026, our monthly distributions returned to Jeremy Henwood Park beginning in March, in partnership with I Love a Clean San Diego, our dedicated community collaborator, for our relaunch event — now expanded with music, performances, and a community-centered Art Exchange.

Questions?

Food Distribution
FAQs

Everything you need to know before you visit. Can't find your answer? Reach out to us directly at info@ascendtials.org.

What is the ASCENDtials food distribution?
ASCENDtials is committed to supporting our community with fresh, nutritious food. On the second Friday of each month, residents can receive fresh fruits, vegetables, and dry goods — completely free of cost.
Who is eligible to receive food?
The distribution is open to all! We believe everyone deserves access to fresh, nutritious food. No restrictions, no requirements — just good food for everyone in our community.
Can I apply for food assistance at the distribution?
Yes, we have community resources on-site where you can apply for these services.
Where can I find food distribution locations?
We are located at Officer Jeremy Henwood Memorial Park, across from our weekly Farmers' Market.
What types of food are available?
We always have a variety of fresh produce, dry goods, and some specialty items.
How does food distribution work?
Patrons line up in the designated location in the park. Be prepared to register and receive bagged items, then either drive off or walk off. Items are handled by volunteers from the community, staff, or partner organizations.
Can I volunteer or donate?
Individuals or businesses can volunteer by signing up or using our application above. We will reach out as soon as possible.
Is there a cost for receiving food?
Food is completely free!
How is the food sourced?
The food comes from donations, government programs, sponsors, and our partnerships with local farms.
What should I bring when picking up food?
We encourage you to bring a carrying bag to transport your goods.
Is there a volunteer sign-up process?
Yes! Individuals or businesses can volunteer by signing up or using our volunteer application. Once submitted, we will reach out as soon as possible. Partner organizations can also fulfill service hours at our events.
Can organizations fulfill service hours at the distribution?
Absolutely. We welcome partner organizations looking to fulfill community service hours. Fill out the volunteer intake form and liability waiver to get started, and we'll be in touch with next steps.
How can I donate food or supplies to the distribution?
We accept non-perishable items, fresh produce, and packaged meals. You can drop off donations at our distribution events or contact us at info@ascendtials.org to arrange a drop-off. Monetary donations and sponsorships are also welcome — every contribution directly impacts the families we serve.
