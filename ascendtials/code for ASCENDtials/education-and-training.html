Our Approach

A holistic ecosystem
of learning

"We seek Oneness that builds capacity and environmental care — ensuring communities have the tools they need to thrive."

ASCENDtials offers learning experiences that reconnect participants with land, culture, and community while building practical skills for personal and professional growth. Our programs span ecological literacy, creative economy training, workforce development, and community-based service learning.

Our unique curriculum, Designing from Whiteness to Oneness™, shifts the focus toward empowering Black communities through dialogue and discussion — challenging oppressive structures while building new ways of thinking.
