Program Overview

A structured, educational pathway

ASCENDtials provides a structured, educational, and community-centered pathway for individuals completing court-ordered community service hours. This program supports personal growth, environmental stewardship, and community accountability while offering courts and partner agencies a reliable, transparent service option.

Participants engage in meaningful work featuring a trauma-informed, culturally rooted curriculum that supports our mission to empower communities through education, sustainability, and civic engagement.

This program requires completion of an intake process before service begins.

Ideal Participants & Eligibility

Who we accept

ASCENDtials accepts individuals with non-violent, low-level offenses. Our program is highly ideal for: youth on probation, adults needing community service hours, diversion program participants, school-based referrals, and individuals needing structured, supervised hours.

Accepted Cases
  • Traffic violations
  • Infractions
  • Misdemeanors (non-violent)
  • Failure to appear (case dependent)
  • Other low-level offenses as approved
Not Accepted
  • Violent offenses
  • Sexual offenses
  • Arson
  • Crimes involving weapons
  • Any offense deemed incompatible with our safety standards
Service Opportunities & Activities

Hands-on tracks in the community

Participants may complete hours through various hands-on community tracks. Assignments depend on availability, skill level, and program needs.

Environmental Cleanups & Gardening

Beach and park cleanups, garden maintenance, urban greening, and ecological stewardship projects across San Diego's waterfront and community green spaces.

Market Service & Community Events

Supporting ASCENDtials' certified farmers' markets, food distribution sites, and community events — setup, logistics, vendor coordination, and direct public engagement.

Cultural Education

Drumming, astronomy sessions, cooking, and culturally rooted programming that connects participants to community heritage and practical life skills.

Youth & Family Programming

Supporting youth and family programs as supervised assistants — garden learning stations, wellness sessions, and community enrichment activities.

Workforce Development & Admin

Administrative support, data entry, outreach, and workforce development tasks that build transferable professional skills under staff supervision.

Program Requirements

What participants must do

All participants are expected to meet the following requirements to remain in good standing and have their hours count toward their court obligation.

  • Complete the intake form and provide required documentation
  • Pay the onboarding donation (or request a waiver)
  • Attend orientation
  • Follow ASCENDtials' Code of Conduct
  • Track hours through the approved system
  • Participate in weekly check-ins
  • Communicate schedule changes promptly
  • Complete all assigned tasks responsibly and respectfully
Verification & Reporting

Documented hours, court-ready

ASCENDtials provides official documentation for all completed service. All hours must be logged through the designated tracking system to be counted toward your court requirement.

Verified community service hours
Certificates of completion
Attendance records
Direct communication with courts or probation officers upon request
Partner Organization Info

Refer a client, partner with us

Courts, probation departments, attorneys, and social service agencies may refer clients to ASCENDtials for community service completion. We provide a safe, structured, and community-centered environment for successful service completion.

To establish a partnership, agencies may contact ASCENDtials directly for agreements, referral procedures, and program materials.

Reliable documentation and verified hours
We provide all records courts need — certificates, attendance logs, and direct officer communication.
Safe, supervised environment with flexible scheduling
All participants are supervised by staff. Scheduling accommodates work, school, and family obligations.
Strong outcomes for youth and families
Our community-centered, restorative approach leads to meaningful outcomes and reduced recidivism.
