Good To Know

Questions people ask before joining

Most often asked questions about Dental Card, with answers.

Is the Dental Card the same as dental insurance?

No. It's not an insurance policy - there's no claims process, no annual cap and no waiting period. You pay a membership fee, and in exchange you get a fixed discount on treatment at our partner clinics.

How much can I actually save?

Savings go up to 50% off standard pricing, depending on the treatment. Call us and we'll talk you through what your specific treatment plan would cost with the card.

Which clinics accept the card?

Only clinics that have partnered with us - not every dentist in Ireland accepts the card. See the current list under Our Partners, or call 089 602 86 89 and we'll confirm the closest one to you.

Can I add my children or partner?

Yes - the family plan covers a partner and children on one card, so the whole household gets the same discounted pricing.

Is there a waiting period before I can use it?

No. Once you're signed up, you can book your first discounted visit straight away - there's no qualifying period like you'd find with insurance.

What happens at my first appointment?

You show your Dental Card at check-in, the clinic applies your discount to the treatment plan, and you pay the discounted price directly - no forms to send off afterwards.
