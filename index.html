About Me

Hi! I'm Yana, a UX/UI designer with 2+ years of experience creating user-centered digital experiences that drive business results.

International Design Perspective
My unique approach to UX/UI stems from a rare opportunity: earning one of only two International Diplomatic Scholarships awarded per country for a full university education in South Korea. This prestigious scholarship led me to spend six years immersing myself in Eastern design philosophies and user behaviors.

This cross-cultural foundation taught me to: - Design for diverse user groups with different cultural expectations - Balance aesthetics and usability across Eastern and Western markets - Understand how cultural context influences user interaction patterns - Create interfaces that resonate globally while respecting local nuances

Today, I bring this international perspective to every project, designing solutions that work for users across European and Asian markets.

My Design Approach I believe the best designs arise from genuine user needs. My process combines: - In-depth user research (interviews, usability testing, A/B testing) - Data-driven design decisions based on user feedback - Iterative prototyping in Figma with focus on usability and accessibility - Close collaboration with developers to ensure accurate implementation

Recent Impact
- Led ASCENDtials platform redesign, achieving 20% increase in subscription conversion - Conducted user research identifying key pain points that guided design strategy - Created design systems ensuring consistency across digital products - Collaborated with cross-functional teams using Agile methodology

Now based in Europe with EU work authorization, I'm excited to bring my international perspective and user-centered approach to innovative companies across Europe.
